# Integration 10 | Source-based live AC modifier foundation

Private Integration 09-derived candidate. Embedded SRD 5.2.1: Shield p.161; Shield of Faith p.162; same-spell effects overlap rather than add. Existing attribution retained.

## Implemented scope
- `dist/lib/srd-live-modifiers.mjs`: typed, fixed-rule incoming modifier ledger (`modifierEffects`, `modifierSequence`), stable local IDs, validated other-caster labels, 24-source engineering cap, fail-closed inputs, no arbitrary numeric grants. Equal Shield of Faith applications overlap; different Shield and Shield of Faith bonuses combine. Existing `shieldEffect` remains in its original format. `liveArmorClass` aggregates those two supported sources over the existing derived AC, and explains the sources. The earlier Shield-only helper remains for compatibility; do not use it as general live AC.
- `modifier-add` and `modifier-remove` use existing Play revision/Undo pipeline. A received Shield of Faith does not consume target slots or action, and losing target Concentration does not end another caster's spell. The player confirms the caster actually applied the spell and it remains active. Duplicate labels for the same spell are rejected (case insensitive); names are local labels, not authenticated character IDs. Successful removal checks exact ID, spell and caster. Ledger counter survives removal. Revision CAS remains essential after Undo.
- The Play health panel and Shield triggering-hit preview both use the shared live AC. No automatic damage change, no magical hit inference, no base-sheet/PDF adjustment. UI is EN/ID with escaped source labels and explicit confirmation. Offline cache revision advanced.
- Shield of Faith remains table-managed for duration and caster Concentration; remove when caster loses Concentration, maximum ten-minute duration ends, or another confirmed end event occurs. Completed Short/Long Rests expire all previously recorded currently supported incoming sources because a completed rest exceeds their maximum duration; record chronology correctly, adding any new cast after the rest separately. This cleanup must be revisited before supporting longer-duration profiles.
- Only incoming Shield of Faith from another caster supported; self-cast, spell-slot granting and cross-character synchronization not implemented. Neither spell establishes a universal AC stacking engine, armor alternatives, transformed stats, inventory-dependent base AC, other D20 modifiers or global timing. Confirm actual AC at table. Older builds may reject new ledger fields, including empty retained sequence; no IndexedDB migration introduced.
