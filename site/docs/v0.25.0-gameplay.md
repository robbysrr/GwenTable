# GwenTable v0.25.0: Rogue and Weapon Mastery gameplay implementation

**Status:** Local QA candidate only. Applies directly over v0.24.9 source; not a production deployment.

## Mechanical reference

- SRD 5.2.1 / 2024 Rogue Sneak Attack: a Finesse or Ranged weapon attack roll hit, Advantage OR an eligible non-Incapacitated ally within 5 feet of target and no Disadvantage; once per creature turn, Rogue level's Sneak Attack table (1d6 at 1-2, +1d6 each two levels). When critical, roll the remaining bonus damage dice twice.
- Level 5 Rogue Cunning Strike: optional one-d6 damage die cost for Poison, Trip or Withdraw, paid *before* critical-dice doubling. Poison requires Poisoner's Kit on person and DC 8 + Dexterity modifier + proficiency for Constitution save. Trip requires Large-or-smaller target and Dexterity save against same DC. Withdraw is a half-Speed movement reminder. The target's save, conditions and hit points are never mutated.
- Rogue Uncanny Dodge at level 5: Reaction when a seen attacker hits with an attack roll; halve incoming damage, rounding down, before applying Temp HP and self HP changes. Concentration saving throw is queued on damage actually taken after halving, including damage absorbed by Temp HP; zero damage creates no check.
- Mastery activation is **not** inferred merely from weapon type. The character's saved Weapon Mastery choice must actually include the weapon. Graze on a miss records the nonnegative attacking ability modifier as separate damage to apply to the target; Nick is an extra-attack timing reminder, not an extra roll. Vex, Slow, Sap, Push, Cleave and Topple are conditional notes; Topple DC is derived; size, damage and target save are table decisions. No NPC record is modified.

Reference pages: [D&D 2024 Rogue features](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes) and [SRD 5.2.1 equipment/mastery](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/library/5e/dd-srd-5-2-1/equipment). Official SRD PDF: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf .

## Architecture and persistence

1. Pure `dist/lib/rogue-strike.js`: validates Rogue, eligible built-in known weapon, effective attack mode, ally eligibility, per-turn marker and Cunning Strike input; calculates bonus dice after cost. Custom weapons explicitly remain DM-adjudicated rather than auto-approved.
2. Pure `dist/lib/weapon-mastery.js`: handles selected mastery only and separates target effects from the PC's state.
3. `dist/lib/combat-assist.js`: extends existing deterministic attack plan/resolve/summary. New optional inputs default to no Sneak Attack for all old attack callers. Damage dice must match plan and may not be forged.
4. `dist/lib/play.js`: tracks `combat.sneakUsed` with a zero-data-loss legacy default. It resets on personal Turn Start and a DM-confirmed external new-turn event, not merely Turn End. Rogue Uncanny Dodge works through the normal atomic Play update path and Undo.
5. `dist/play-sheet.js`: adds explicit Rogue eligibility inputs, Cunning Strike fields, other-creature turn reset, and Uncanny Dodge form. New strings localized to English and Bahasa Indonesia.
6. `dist/sw.js`: registers both new pure modules and isolates the v0.25.0 offline cache from older builds. Artwork-withheld preview uses a separate cache name.

## Boundaries / limitations

- The app does **not** track full encounter initiative order. A Rogue who has already used Sneak Attack on their turn must explicitly confirm a different creature's turn before applying Sneak Attack outside their own turn. Outside a tracked combat encounter, DM manages the once-per-turn usage.
- Advantage/Disadvantage sources such as conditions, cover, darkness, and external NPC abilities remain player-entered/DM adjudicated.
- A critical hit doubles rolled Sneak Attack dice but not flat damage modifiers. A Cunning Strike die is removed before the doubling. No enemy HP, save, status, movement, or Action/Bonus Action marker is automatically changed.
- Cunning Strike options beyond those confirmed and included in the supported level-1–12 builder are not automated. Rogue levels 13–20 are engine reference inputs only, **not** a claim that high-level Rogue creation has shipped.
- No new IndexedDB schema migration is needed. Older data does not include `combat.sneakUsed` and is normalized to false. Explicitly invalid supplied values fail backup validation. Preserved Play snapshots, private exports and one-step Undo remain supported.
- Not tested: real Chromium, Android, keyboard and assistive technologies, multi-tab UI acceptance, screen reader or public asset rights. Production release remains **NO GO**.
