# GwenTable v0.25.3: Barbarian (Path of the Berserker) high-level slice

**Release scope:** Local development only, 19 November 2026. Follow SRD 5.2.1 (2024), Barbarian pp. 28–30. This is *not* 2014 Barbarian advancement; in particular, level 20 does not give unlimited Rage.

## Implemented

- Barbarian builder now offers levels **1–20** alongside Fighter and Rogue. All other unfinished classes remain limited to 1–12.
- Level 16 Ability Score Improvement / feat and level 19 Epic Boon are explicit persisted validated choices. Character HP, XP, proficiency, weapon mastery and hit dice derive from those choices.
- Level 20 **Primal Champion** adds +4 Strength and Constitution, with a 25 maximum, including **retroactive HP** from the new Constitution modifier across all 20 levels. Existing build schemas are not migrated.
- Accurate SRD Rage uses by level: 2 at 1–2; 3 at 3–5; 4 at 6–11; 5 at 12–16; 6 at 17–20. Rage damage +2 at levels 1–8, +3 at 9–15, +4 at 16–20. Weapon Mastery count 2 at 1–3, 3 at 4–9, 4 at 10–20.
- The progression reference includes level 13 extra Brutal Strike options; level 14 Path of the Berserker **Intimidating Presence**; level 15 **Persistent Rage**; level 17 2d10 and two different Brutal Strike effects; level 18 **Indomitable Might** minimum for Strength checks and Strength saves (not attack rolls); level 20 Primal Champion.
- Play Sheet: Intimidating Presence requires confirmation and a Bonus Action during combat, tracks once-per-Long-Rest usage, and can be recharged by spending one Rage use (no action required). Wisdom save DC = 8 + Strength modifier + proficiency. The target saves, duration and Frightened outcome remain DM-controlled.
- Play Sheet: at level 15+, a player may choose **Persistent Rage** as part of an Initiative roll, once per Long Rest. It refills spent Rage uses; it cannot be used while the character has all uses remaining. Short Rest does not recharge this once-per-Long-Rest feature.
- English and Bahasa Indonesia copy; source service-worker cache version `open-5e-v0.25.3` with new pure `barbarian-progression.js` precached. Existing flag toggle, private backup/Undo, and legacy Play state are preserved. Two new counters default safely when importing legacy records.
- The previously static Rage label at level 3 now shows its correct 3-use progression.

## What has NOT been automated

- Rage **active duration**, Rage resistances, Reckless Attack timing, Frenzy extra damage, Relentless Rage DC history, special Brutal Strike attack rolls and effects, and enemy condition changes are still decided at the table. These are not claimed as fully automatic.
- The separate personal turn counter does not model a full initiative order, encounter rounds, creatures' saves, target HP, or battlefield geometry.
- Level 13–20 for Bard, Cleric, Druid, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock and Wizard remain capped at 12. A full class-wide unlock would be mechanically premature.
- Source validation and unit tests do not constitute real browser or mobile acceptance. No production repository, deployment, or user data changed.

## Test scope

New `tests/barbarian-high-level-srd.test.js` contains **12 tests**, including 800 seeded builds for levels 13–20, level boundary assertions, Primal Champion/HP, class features, invalid actions, Bonus Action timing, Rage recharge, Short/Long Rest, legacy-state validation, single-character backup, and Play UI/cache references.

Baseline v0.25.2: **430** passing. Candidate v0.25.3: **442 passing** (0 failures). Run `npm test`, `npm run build`, `npm run verify:source`, `npm run build:artwork-withheld-preview`, `npm run audit:artwork-withheld-preview`.
