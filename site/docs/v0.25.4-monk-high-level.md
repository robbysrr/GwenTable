# GwenTable v0.25.4: Monk (Warrior of the Open Hand), level 13–20

Local implementation on top of v0.25.3. Rules source: D&D SRD 5.2.1, Monk class and Warrior of the Open Hand, https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf ; readable official rules: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes . This is **2024 rules**, not the 2014 Monk.

## Implementation

- `dist/lib/monk-progression.js`: pure, range-validated levels 1–20 table for Martial Arts dice (d6/d8/d10/d12), Focus Points, Unarmored Movement bonuses (10/15/20/25/30 feet), and high-level thresholds. `perfectFocusSpent` takes a *spent* count and returns the proper *spent* count after Initiative.
- `dist/lib/character.js`: Monk joins Fighter, Rogue and Barbarian with builder levels 1–20. Level 16 feat/ASI and level 19 Epic Boon remain explicit validated choices. Level 20 Body and Mind adds +4 Dexterity and Wisdom, maximum 25. All derived attacks, AC, initiative, spell/skill modifiers, and HP are still calculated from the character. Level 14 Disciplined Survivor applies proficiency to all six saving throws; no duplicate proficiency. Level 17 Martial Arts d12 is shown in feature text and attack stats. Movement increases at Monk levels 14 and 18 are correctly applied when not wearing armor or a shield.
- `dist/lib/play.js`: Focus pool stays equal to Monk level. Perfect Focus triggers on Initiative only if not using Uncanny Metabolism and 3 or fewer points remain, bringing remaining Focus to 4. Disciplined Survivor consumes 1 point after the player confirms a failed saving throw and supplies a new d20 result; the new result must be used.
- Quivering Palm: confirms an Unarmed Strike hit, costs 4 Focus, stores only one target (1–60 character private label), can be dismissed harmlessly, and can be resolved with exactly ten d12 results. The damage total and half-on-success total are logged. A resolve-via-Action marks the character's Action in active combat, while the alternative of foregoing one Attack-action attack remains DM-controlled and does not silently consume an entire Action. A player must confirm being on the target's plane. Duration in days and enemy HP/saves are **not** automated. Quivering target state is included in private Play backups, reconciliation and one-step Undo; malformed supplied data is rejected, and older saves without the field are compatible.
- Superior Defense is intentionally **reference-only**, with a warning that the DM tracks its one-minute duration and Resistance. It does not silently apply damage resistance or spend Focus automatically.
- Play Sheet controls are available in English and Indonesian. Offline cache revised to `open-5e-v0.25.4` and precaches the new module. Existing flag language toggle, PDF/GCX, SRD compendium, roster and draft protections are unchanged.

## Explicit limits

This is a verified **builder progression vertical slice**, not a production guarantee or a full encounter simulator. A DM still confirms attack hits, enemy saves and HP, target identification/duration, Monk's optional Open Hand effects, Superior Defense timing/Resistance, and the other unsimulated class mechanics. Real browser, IndexedDB/multi-tab, mobile, screen-reader, PDF, GCX and deployment tests have not been executed in this local pass. Original 12 art files remain uncleared. No production deployment.
