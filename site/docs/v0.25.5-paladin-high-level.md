# GwenTable v0.25.5: Paladin (Oath of Devotion), levels 13–20

Implementation on v0.25.4. Rules basis: SRD 5.2.1 (CC BY 4.0), official Paladin 2024 class and Oath of Devotion tables, https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/2190881-paladin. This is **not** the 2014 Paladin subclass.

## Implemented

- `dist/lib/paladin-progression.js`: pure validated 1–20 level table with prepared spells, spell-slot counts, Channel Divinity uses and aura radius. Fully distinguishes normal prepared spell selections from always-prepared Oath spells.
- `dist/lib/character.js`: Paladin joins Fighter, Rogue, Barbarian and Monk with builder levels 1–20; 16th-level feat/ASI and 19th-level Epic Boon choices remain explicit and validated. 4th-level slots start at Paladin 13 and 5th-level slots at Paladin 17. Prepared spell counts reach 15 by Paladin 19. Oath spells: Freedom of Movement and Guardian of Faith at 13; Commune and Flame Strike at 17. The latter are always prepared and do not consume the Paladin preparation budget.
- Feature thresholds: Restoring Touch 14; Smite of Protection (Oath) 15; Aura Expansion 18 (30-foot Emanation); Holy Nimbus 20.
- `dist/lib/play.js`: Restoring Touch removes one or more specified Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Stunned conditions for **5 Lay On Hands points each**. It consumes the Bonus Action during a tracked turn, updates the self sheet when the character actually has the condition, and leaves ally conditions/HP to the DM. Invalid or ineligible requests do not commit state.
- Holy Nimbus starts with Bonus Action if a use remains, records a manual active state (not a timer), can be ended with no action, and can be recharged without action by spending one remaining level 5 spell slot. A Short or Long Rest ends the tracked aura; Long Rest restores use. The UI displays radiant damage `Charisma modifier + proficiency`, sunlight and Holy Ward reminders, not calculated target HP. It does not simulate the ten-minute clock or automatically disable enemy effects.
- Level-15+ Divine Smite logs the Oath of Devotion Smite of Protection Half Cover reminder. The engine does **not** apply cover automatically to the entire party. Private Play backups and reconciliation carry the new `nimbus` field; legacy backups without it remain valid. Malformed supplied fields are rejected.
- `dist/play-sheet.js` and `dist/locale-copy.js`: bilingual English/Indonesian controls, native buttons/forms, 44px target styling inherited from existing UI. Offline service-worker revision v0.25.5 includes the new pure rule module.

## Verification

- Source baseline: v0.25.4, 459 passing tests. Candidate: **476/476** passing (17 new) including 800 generated Paladin builds level 13–20.
- Static build passes. Artwork-withheld preview passes audit with zero unapproved image binaries; the normal development build remains blocked by 12 uncleared artwork files.
- SHA-256 integrity, patch reproduction and separate ZIP extraction are checked in the handoff. Real-browser, mobile, assistive-technology, download and staging behavior **not verified**.

## Known limits / explicit non-claims

- Feature unlocks and state transitions have Node coverage, not real mouse/touch/keyboard acceptance.
- Holy Nimbus timer, targets and Divine Smite's ally Half Cover effect are DM-managed; the app does not pretend to track individual enemies.
- Only five classes have complete *builder level-select paths* to 20: Fighter, Rogue, Barbarian, Monk, Paladin. Seven other classes remain capped at level 12. Having a level-20 builder path is not equivalent to complete simulation of all class-specific features.
- The save-schema is extended compatibly with an optional `nimbus` field but no IndexedDB migration. Copies remain private.
- **G1 REPAIR / PUBLIC NO GO.** Publication rights and owner signoff are not granted by QA preview generation.
