# GwenTable v0.25.6 · Ranger/Hunter level 13–20 (local only)

**Status:** Sprint 02 G1 REPAIR; PUBLIC NO GO. This is an SRD 5.2.1 rules-engine increment; it is not evidence of successful browser, mobile, or accessibility acceptance.

## Scope and implementation

- Full **Ranger/Hunter level 1–20 builder path**, retaining all existing choices, selected Weapon Masteries, and saved records. Level 13–20 has validated spell preparation, half-caster slots, level 16 feat/ASI, level 19 Epic Boon, XP, Hit Dice, and proficiency.
- `dist/lib/ranger-progression.js` defines the independently testable Ranger table. 4th-level slots begin at Ranger 13; 5th-level slots begin at Ranger 17. Prepared-spell counts reach 15 at Ranger 19–20. Free Hunter’s Mark uses scale 2/3/4/5/6 at class levels 1/5/9/13/17.
- **Relentless Hunter, level 13:** correctly ignores Concentration saving throws caused by damage to the active *Hunter’s Mark*. The engine still ends Concentration on Incapacitation, unconsciousness and other normal ending conditions. A different Concentration spell still requires a check. Common straight/curly apostrophe spellings are recognized.
- **Nature’s Veil, level 14:** dedicated Bonus Action control, Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) uses per Long Rest, optional personal combat marker, Undo and import/export compatibility. The 2024 Invisible condition ends at the end of the character’s next turn; GwenTable logs the effect and leaves duration tracking to the DM rather than changing unrelated invisibility sources automatically.
- **Superior Hunter’s Defense, level 15:** on confirmed damage, spend Reaction, halve one specified damage type (round down), apply Temporary HP and HP, and schedule any applicable Concentration check. The resistance continuing through the current turn is a DM reminder, **not** an automatic permanent resistance flag. Do not record the same incoming damage in the normal HP form again. Other resistances and vulnerabilities require DM confirmation.
- **Precise Hunter, level 17:** when a player explicitly confirms that the attacked creature is affected by their currently concentrated *Hunter’s Mark*, the attack planner grants Advantage (subject to normal cancellation with Disadvantage). No target linkage is invented.
- **Feral Senses, level 18:** Blindsight 30 feet in the derived rules reference; environmental/DM adjudication remains external.
- **Foe Slayer, level 20:** a confirmed hit on an explicitly marked target rolls d10 rather than d6 bonus damage from Hunter’s Mark. Critical hits roll **two** bonus dice, while flat modifiers remain undoubled. The bonus is included *once* in the logged total, never applied to external HP automatically.
- English/Indonesian UI copy, separate artwork-withheld cache revision, unchanged private storage schema and versioned backups.

## Known limitations

- Other classes still capped at 12: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard. Level-20 path does not imply every feat or spell interaction is automated.
- Hunter’s Mark target selection is deliberately explicit and per attack. The app does not own target state, all conditions, damage resistance stacking, or the global combat clock.
- The personal turn tracker is not a full initiative system; Hunter’s Defense’s continuing resistance and Nature’s Veil expiry require DM adjudication.
- The Ranger attack engine logs target damage; it does not deduct enemy HP or automatically spend an Action.
- This ZIP contains inherited local artwork; do not publish directly. Separate artwork-withheld preview is for QA only.
